# Showdesk · one-pager

**Headline:** A ringside desk that keeps the class, the review, and the report as one record.

**Problem:** Show secretaries retype results. Judges argue from memory. Monday’s report is a different document.

**Solution:** Phone at ringside, review on the same line, placements and reports from the same desk.

**Why Showdesk**
- The phone is the only new surface for the person in the ring.
- Review is the class slowed down, not a second app.
- The PDF is an export, not a rewrite.
