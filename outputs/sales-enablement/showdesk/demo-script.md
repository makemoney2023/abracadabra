# Showdesk · first demo script (30 min)

Tied to footage in `outputs/videos/showdesk/` and the live site.

**Goal:** Show three workflows that match their event pain. Book a technical follow-up or a paid desk trial.

## Opening (4 min)

Recap their last show: who sat ringside, who reviewed placements, who needed a report the next morning. Confirm those three still matter.

## Workflow 1. Ringside (8 min)

**Pain:** notes and results live in someone’s head or a paper card.  
**Show:** `demo-ringside.mp4` / live ringside. Phone in hand, live line, mocked STT if demo mode.  
**Talk track:** “This is the secretary’s phone during the class. The desk hears the same line.”  
**Ask:** “Who is holding the phone at your show today?”

## Workflow 2. Review (7 min)

**Pain:** disputes after the class, no shared tape.  
**Show:** `demo-review.mp4`.  
**Talk track:** “Review is the same class, slowed down, with the line still attached.”  
**Ask:** “Who has to defend a placement on Monday?”

## Workflow 3. Placements and reports (7 min)

**Pain:** results trapped in one laptop.  
**Show:** `demo-desk.mp4` then the reports chapter of `showdesk-promo.mp4`.  
**Talk track:** “Placements and the report are the same record. You do not retype the class.”  
**Ask:** “Who gets the PDF today, and how late?”

## Close (4 min)

Recommend a one-show trial. Name the secretary, the judge chair, and the report owner. Send the promo and this script the same day.
