# LLM Leverage · one-pager

**Headline:** A self-paced course that trains a team to get reliable AI answers.

**Problem:** Teams prompt in private chats, copy bad answers into real work, and have no shared practice.

**Solution:** Twelve modules on the context loop, gated quizzes, workshops that match the same curriculum.

**Why the course**
- Tool-agnostic. Maps across ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot.
- Completion is gated at 75% on the quiz.
- Workshops are the same source of truth, not a second syllabus.

**Proof on film:** `outputs/footage/llmcourse-home-lesson.webm`. Live: https://llm-leverage-course.vercel.app/

**Next step:** Preview Mental model, then plan a four-session rollout.
