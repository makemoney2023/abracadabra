# LLMCourse · team-rollout demo script (25 min)

Tied to `outputs/footage/llmcourse-home-lesson.webm`, `llmcourse-workshops.webm`, and https://llm-leverage-course.vercel.app/.

**Buyer:** an operator buying a team rollout, not a single learner.

## Opening (4 min)

Ask how the team currently writes prompts, and where bad answers have already cost them. Confirm whether they want self-paced, workshop, or both.

## Workflow 1. Preview a module (8 min)

Home → Preview the course → Mental model. Mark three steps done. Show Practice, then the quiz gate (75%).  
**Talk track:** “Completion is gated. A certificate is not a click-through.”  
**Ask:** “Who in your team would fail this gate today?”

## Workflow 2. Workshops (6 min)

`/workshops` → Session 1 slides → Next slide. Point at linked modules and the try-it sandbox.  
**Talk track:** “The in-class series is the same curriculum, not a second course.”

## Workflow 3. Rollout and contact (5 min)

Back to home `#rollout` and `#contact`.  
**Talk track:** “Progress is local in v1. A team rollout is how you get shared practice, not how you log in.”

## Close (2 min)

Leave the Preview link and this script. Offer a four-session workshop calendar, not a seat count.
