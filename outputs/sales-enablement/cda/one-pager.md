# Canadian Discount Appliances · one-pager

**Headline:** The appliance warehouse, and the question before you drive.

**Problem:** Outlet pricing is a game. The customer has three tabs open and a doorway they have not measured.

**Solution:** One warehouse page. Three conditions with plain-word warranties, $99 next-business-day delivery, and a Path Check that says whether the fridge gets through the door before the truck leaves.

**Why this site**
- The price is on the tag and the tag is on the machine. No sale that was the price last month.
- New, Unboxed, Used. Three conditions, three warranties, written in plain words.
- Path Check turns "will it fit?" into a verdict, not a guess in the parking lot.
- 4.7 from 137 Google reviews sits next to the address and the phone number.
