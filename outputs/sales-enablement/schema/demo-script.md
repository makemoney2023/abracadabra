# Schema · first demo script (20 min)

Tied to `outputs/footage/schema-home-scan.webm` and https://schema-two.vercel.app/. Internal playbook in the Schema repo (`docs/sales/`) already asks for a 2-minute walkthrough of `/scan/[token]`.

**Positioning:** sell AI visibility, not schema markup.

## Opening (3 min)

Name their city and niche. Ask whether ChatGPT or Perplexity would recommend them today. Do not mention JSON-LD yet.

## Workflow 1. The scan (6 min)

Paste their URL. Wait for the score and top gaps.  
**Talk track:** “This is how answer engines see the site. The score is the translation layer, not the writing.”  
**Ask:** “Which competitor do you lose citations to?”

## Workflow 2. The fix package (6 min)

Open generate-fix. Show the Organization tree and the install path.  
**Talk track:** “We do not redesign the site. We add the machine-readable layer the engines already know how to read.”

## Workflow 3. Ops inbox (optional, 3 min)

If they buy the studio motion: show the ops queue, not the public page. Staff scan, notes, next send.

## Close (2 min)

Send their scan URL. Book a follow-up after they unlock the PDF. Attach `proposal-tiers` language from the Schema repo, not prices on a public page.
