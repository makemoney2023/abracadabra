# 7-Day AI Blind Spot Nurture (4 emails)

Plain-text, one-to-one tone. Personalize with company, city/niche, competitor, and their Schema scan URL.

**Base URL:** `{APP_URL}/scan/{token}`  
**Send as:** Consultant voice — not HTML marketing templates.  
**Email 1 tip:** If possible, embed a Loom thumbnail pointing at *their* Schema score.

## Email 2 — Day 3: Translation layer (education)

**Subject:** The “translation layer” missing from [Company Name]

```
Hi [Name],

I shared a Schema report a couple of days ago on why AI engines struggle to read [Company Name]. Quick analogy:

Humans can read your site fine. AI looks for a hidden translation layer (structured data and related signals). Without it, you’re handing ChatGPT a 10-page brochure and asking it to find your phone number. With it, you’re handing a business card — services, location, and facts with high certainty.

AI hates guessing, so it prioritizes sites that make those facts easy.

Is fixing this AI blind spot a priority for your team this quarter?

Report again if useful: [Scan URL]

Best,
[Your Name]
```
