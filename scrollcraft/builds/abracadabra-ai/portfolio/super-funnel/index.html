SuperPatch · Field app for associates

Every link an associate shares ends in a credited, followed-up lead.

Super Funnel System is a field app for SuperPatch associates. It takes a stranger from a shared link, through a quiz or a recruiting page, into a nine-step pipeline. It then hands the associate the exact message to send next. It runs on Cloudflare Workers, backed by Postgres and R2.

The flow

One lead, start to finish.

The clearest way to explain the product is to follow a single lead.

  1. 01

    An associate signs in once.

    They use their existing backoffice username and password. The app builds a profile from their rep ID and shop username. Every contact, page, and post they create belongs to that profile and no one else’s.

  2. 02

    They share one link.

    It can be their digital card, a QR code, a recruiting page, or a product funnel. Each one carries the associate’s rep code. When a visitor arrives through someone else’s link, the page they are reading still pays the page owner.

  3. 03

    The visitor picks a door.

    • The product funnel answers one question: is there a patch for my problem? It collects nothing. It sends the visitor to the Signal Score quiz with the owner’s credit attached.
    • The recruiting page answers a different question: should I build with this person? It tells the associate’s story, shows proof and testimonials, and ends in a lead form. After the form, it offers a booking link.
  4. 04

    The lead lands in the pipeline.

    A quiz unlock or a form submission creates a contact with no second form and no re-entry. A quiz lead arrives with its recommended patches, a link back to the report, and a source tag. A lead who wants to build a business enters at the Recruit step. A product lead enters at the first step. If the same lead arrives twice, the app recognises it and creates no duplicate. If the app is unreachable, the visitor still gets their report.

  5. 05

    The associate works it.

    Contacts move through nine steps: Add Contact, Rapport, Discovery, Samples, Follow-up, Close, Purchase Links, Testimonial, Recruit. Each step carries its own script. The associate switches between a searchable list and a drag-and-drop board and filters by lead date. They can import contacts from CSV, vCard, or the phone’s contact picker.

  6. 06

    The follow-up leaves from their own phone.

    The script opens as a text, email, or WhatsApp draft already addressed to the contact. The associate reads it and presses send. One tap on I sent it logs the message and advances the contact. Bulk mode queues drafts for many contacts and opens them one at a time. The app never sends a message by itself.

  7. 07

    Home tells them what to do next.

    The top of the screen shows hot and new leads ranked by intent: landing leads first, then sample requests, then report-only quiz leads. Below that are the follow-ups due today and a setup checklist for new associates. The widgets for survey activity, samples, and lead locations can be rearranged.

What’s inside

The tools around the pipeline.

  • Sample queue

    A quiz visitor who asks for a sample appears in a ship queue. The associate marks the sample shipped. They can export the list to CSV or Excel for fulfilment.

  • My Page and My Funnel

    Associates edit their public pages without touching code: story, photos, city, video, testimonials, and which sections appear. The recruiting page is a chaptered scroll experience built around film and animation. Phones get their own lighter media. Visitors who prefer reduced motion get still images.

  • Digital card

    The card has a photo, social links, an Add to Contacts download, and a QR code. It generates its own preview image, so it looks right wherever it is shared.

  • Social studio

    Associates write captions that pass brand-voice and compliance checks, schedule posts on a calendar, and read performance analytics. One inbox covers Instagram, Facebook, and X, with keyword auto-replies. Every reply passes the same banned-phrase check as a new post.

  • Compliance

    A Say This / Never Say This guide and a copy checker keep every message inside company rules.

  • Team

    Leaders see the people they personally enrolled.

  • Guided onboarding

    The first visit to each screen plays a short walkthrough video. A replay button stays in the top bar.

Built on Cloudflare

One Worker, one deployment, no servers to manage.

The whole app runs as one Cloudflare Worker, with no servers to manage. Next.js is compiled for Workers through OpenNext with Node compatibility enabled. Pages, API routes, webhooks, and scheduled jobs all run at the edge, close to the associate and the visitor.

  • Neon Postgres through Hyperdrive

    Workers can’t hold database connections open, so Hyperdrive pools them. Contacts, profiles, page configurations, journeys, and social data all live in Postgres. The pgvector extension is installed and ready for AI retrieval features. If the database is not configured, the app fails closed and the health check reports it. It never quietly falls back to storing data somewhere else.

  • R2 for files

    Associate photos and page media sit in a private bucket, served only through an authenticated media route. A second bucket holds the Next.js incremental cache.

  • Cron for the social studio

    An hourly scheduled job syncs post analytics, follower counts, and inbox history. It calls the app’s own sync route through a service binding, so the job runs inside the same deployment and uses the same code.

  • A separate Worker for the quiz

    Signal Score stays its own deployment. The two systems talk through one shared-secret webhook. This keeps a problem on one side from taking down the other. A lead is recorded once per quiz session, so a retried webhook never creates a duplicate.

  • Rate limits in Postgres

    Login and public lead forms are throttled with an atomic fixed-window counter in the database. It holds under concurrent requests from many edge locations, where a read-then-write counter would let bursts through.

  • Tenancy

    Every contact, page, and post is tied to a profile ID. An associate can reach only their own records.

  • Release discipline

    • Every push runs type checks, lint, and the full test suite in CI.
    • A migration runner applies schema changes in order and records a checksum for each one. It refuses to re-apply a file that was edited after it ran.
    • Secrets live in Worker secrets, never in the repo.
    • The deploy command forces local-prototype flags off so a development setting can’t ship to production.

Designed for the phone

Most associates work from a phone, so the app starts there.

  • It installs to the home screen.
  • The contact sheet is a full-height swipeable carousel on mobile.
  • Every tap target is at least 44 px.
  • Public pages meet accessibility standards and load fast on mobile connections.

Outcome

One link in, one credited contact out.

An associate shares one link. A stranger becomes a contact, credited to the right person, with a script ready to send. Every public call to action pays the page owner. The associate sends every message themselves. The whole system runs on one Cloudflare deployment with a single sign-in.
