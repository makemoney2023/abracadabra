The brief

Build something useful enough to earn the search.

pirx.ca is the app and the blogs. The app is the product people use. The blogs are the public pages behind the search record below.

The product gives runners a practical race-time predictor. The editorial layer answers the specific questions runners already type into search and AI assistants.

The system

Product, evidence, and useful answers—one connected experience.

The result

Demand compounded. The evidence stayed attached.

Google Search Console, Jul 18 to Oct 17, 2026, recorded 398K search impressions, 1.94K clicks, and an average position of 7.6. The count starts near zero in mid-July.

The Generative AI features report is a separate number and is not added to the search total. It recorded 46.5K impressions in the same window. The top pages are the blog guides: Good 10K time by age (11,068), Good half marathon time by age (8,192), and Good 5K time by age (6,728).

The AI-feature count is separate and is not added to the search total. Results vary by site; these are pirx.ca’s own dated numbers.
