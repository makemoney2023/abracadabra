The file is there. The answer still takes a person.

Someone asks a question the company has already answered. The reply lives in a handbook, a policy, a price sheet, or a thread from last month. The person who knows which file opens it, scrolls, and pastes the paragraph. Everyone else waits, or they guess.

Search inside a shared drive finds a document, not an answer. You still have to know the file name, and you still have to read it. When the wrong version is newer than the right one, the guess looks official.

What changes

  • A plain question

    Staff or a customer ask the way they already talk. They do not need the file name.

  • The page, not a paraphrase alone

    The answer points at the page it came from. You can open it and see the sentence.

  • The current file

    When you replace a policy, the next answer reads the new file. The old paragraph does not keep winning because it was uploaded first.

  • A stop when it is not in the files

    If the documents do not contain the answer, it says so. It does not fill the gap with a general guess.

Three places it shows up

  • A policy lookup

    Someone asks what the rule is. The answer quotes the policy you keep and links the page, instead of a manager retyping it from memory.

  • A part, a price, or a clause

    The question is specific. The reply comes from the sheet or the contract you already approved, with the line it used.

  • A staff question that should not wait

    The handbook already covers it. The person gets the paragraph and gets back to the job, and the manager is interrupted only when the file is silent.

How it gets built

The order is the same on every service. The job is what changes.

  1. Brief

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moment a person has to say yes. We write that list before anyone builds. A step that is not on the list does not ship.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect it to the records you already keep. The new system should not be a second place to type the same fact.

  3. People approve

    Your team runs last week's real work through it and marks what may go live. We do not put a customer-facing step in front of people on our say-so alone.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live. We watch the first runs with you, fix what the real work shows, and leave the rules where your team can change them.

Questions

Will it answer from the public internet?

No. This service reads the files you give it. Questions that live on the public web belong in search-grounded research, and we keep the two apart.

What if two files disagree?

You pick which file is current. Until you do, the answer shows both pages and does not pretend they say the same thing.

Can customers see internal files?

Only the set you mark for them. A staff handbook and a customer policy are different piles, and the question is asked against one pile.

Does it work on a scan?

If the scan has text we can read, yes. A photo of a page with no text layer is called out, not guessed.

Who updates the files?

You do. We connect the place you already keep them. Replacing a file is the update.

Is the answer legal or financial advice?

No. It shows what your document says. A decision that needs a qualified person still goes to that person.

See it on your work

Bring the file people ask about most, and one question from last week. We will show the answer and the page, or we will show you that the file does not contain it.
