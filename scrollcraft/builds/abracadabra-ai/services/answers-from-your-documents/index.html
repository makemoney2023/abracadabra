Answers from your own documents

What it is

A way to ask a plain question and get an answer from your own files: handbooks, policies, price sheets, manuals. The answer points back to the page it came from.

What it looks like in your business

  • Looking up a part or policy in your own documents
  • Finding the clause a customer is asking about, without opening ten files
  • Answering a staff question from the handbook instead of interrupting a manager

How it works

  1. You give us the documents that are allowed to be searched.
  2. We store them and make them searchable, and we leave out anything that should stay private.
  3. The search runs on Cloudflare. A question comes back with the passage it used.
  4. You check a handful of real questions. If the source is wrong, we fix the file, not the sentence.

What you get

  • A search your team or your customers can ask in plain language
  • Answers that cite the document and the passage
  • A list of which files are in, and which are out
  • A way to replace a file when it changes

Who it's for

Teams that already wrote the answer down, and still spend the day hunting for it.

Signs you need it

  • People ask the same question because the file is hard to find
  • New staff learn by interrupting whoever has been there longest
  • Customers get a different answer depending on who picks up

Built on AI Search, Vectorize, R2. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Will it answer from the open web?

No. It answers from the documents you put in.

What if two documents disagree?

It shows both passages. You decide which file is current, and we retire the old one.

Can customers see internal files?

Only the set you mark for them. Internal files stay on the staff side.

What file types work?

The documents you already send around: PDFs, pages, and spreadsheets saved as files. If a format is awkward, we say so before we load it.
