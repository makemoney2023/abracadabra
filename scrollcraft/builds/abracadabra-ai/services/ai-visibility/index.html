When customers ask AI, make sure it recommends you.

A buyer asks a question you have already answered in public. The reply they see names you, or it names someone else, or it quotes a page you retired. You cannot fix that by arguing with the model. You can shape it by what you publish, which pages you offer to be read, and which crawlers you refuse.

Most sites can't see which AI tools visited last week. We decide which ones may read your site, which stay out, and make sure your best pages are ready to be quoted.

What you get

  • A note in your words

    A plain file tells a reader, human or crawler, what you do and which pages are the ones to cite. It does not invent a claim.

  • You choose who reads your site

    Let search tools in, keep data-harvesting bots out.

  • A page worth quoting

    The public page answers a real question in language you will stand behind. That is the material an answer engine has to work with.

  • A record of who came

    You can see the crawlers that fetched the site. You can read the report again.

Use cases

  • A public page worth quoting

    The page that answers the question buyers already ask is the one the note points at. Older pages that contradict it are not left in the way.

  • A private page that should stay quiet

    A draft, a login, or an internal note is left out of the file and, where it matters, blocked. Public and private are different decisions.

  • A weekly look at who fetched the site

    A weekly view of which AI tools visited, so nothing catches you off guard.

FAQ

Does this guarantee a mention in answers?

No. It makes the page worth quoting and tells crawlers what you want them to read. Being mentioned still depends on the engine.

Will a block hurt search?

A block aimed at a training crawler is a different switch from a search crawler. Which is which is named before anything is blocked. If they cannot be told apart, nothing is blocked.

What is the plain-text note?

A file on the site that introduces the business and lists the pages worth reading. People can read it too. Claims that are not on the site are not hidden there.

Are new pages required?

Only if the current ones do not answer the question. Filler is not published to chase a crawler.

Can another site's numbers appear on yours?

No. The pirx.ca figures stay attached to pirx.ca and to the date they were pulled. The next report belongs to the next domain.

How often do the figures move?

The number on this page is the Google Search Console window Jul 14 to Oct 13, 2026. It is not refreshed from memory.
