AI visibility and crawler control

What it is

A clear view of which automated visitors read the site, plus a plain file that tells answer engines what the business is and which pages matter. You decide what is open and what is off limits.

What it looks like in your business

  • Seeing which crawlers fetched your pages last week
  • Publishing a short llms.txt that points answer engines at the pages you want quoted
  • Blocking a crawler that is taking more than you want to give

How it works

  1. We read how the site is already being fetched.
  2. We write the public note, llms.txt, in your words: what you do, and which pages to read.
  3. The controls run on Cloudflare, so a block or an allow applies before the page is served.
  4. You review the note and the blocks before they go live.

What you get

  • A report of crawler traffic you can re-read
  • An llms.txt, or an update to the one you have
  • Allow and block rules for the crawlers you name
  • A note of what we changed

Who it's for

Owners who want answer engines to quote the right pages, and who want a say in what gets copied.

Signs you need it

  • You do not know which bots are reading the site
  • An answer engine describes you from an old or wrong page
  • You want a public page quoted, and a private one left alone

Questions

Does this guarantee we show up in answers?

No. It tells crawlers what you want them to read, and it shows you who came. Being quoted still depends on the engine.

Will a block break search ranking?

A block aimed at a training crawler is separate from a search crawler. We name which is which before anything is blocked.

What is llms.txt?

A plain text file on the site that introduces the business and lists the pages worth reading. People can read it too.

Do we need new copy?

Only the description you are willing to stand behind. We do not invent claims for the file.
