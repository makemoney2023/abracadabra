An answer engine is describing you from the wrong page

A buyer asks a question you have already answered in public. The reply they see names you, or it names someone else, or it quotes a page you retired. You cannot fix that by shouting at the model. You can fix what you publish, which pages you ask to be read, and which crawlers you refuse.

Most teams do not know who fetched the site last week. A block aimed at the wrong crawler can hide you from search. A wide-open site can hand a private page to a training run. The work is specific: the note, the allow list, the block list, and a page that deserves to be quoted.

What changes

  • A note in your words

    A plain file tells a reader, human or crawler, what you do and which pages are the ones to cite. It does not invent a claim.

  • A say in who crawls

    You can allow a search crawler and refuse a training crawler. We name which is which before anything is blocked.

  • A page worth quoting

    The public page answers a real question in language you will stand behind. That is what an answer engine has to work with.

  • A record of who came

    You can see which crawlers fetched the site. The report is something you can re-read, not a dashboard you have to trust on faith.

Three places it shows up

  • A public page you want quoted

    You choose the page that answers the question buyers already ask. The note points at it. Old pages that contradict it are not left in the way.

  • A private page that should stay quiet

    A draft, a login, or an internal note is left out of the file and, where it matters, blocked. Public and private are different decisions.

  • A weekly look at who fetched the site

    You see the crawlers that came. A surprise visitor is a decision, not a mystery you discover months later.

How it gets built

The order is the same on every service. The job is what changes.

  1. Brief

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moment a person has to say yes. We write that list before anyone builds. A step that is not on the list does not ship.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect it to the records you already keep. The new system should not be a second place to type the same fact.

  3. People approve

    Your team runs last week's real work through it and marks what may go live. We do not put a customer-facing step in front of people on our say-so alone.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live. We watch the first runs with you, fix what the real work shows, and leave the rules where your team can change them.

Questions

Does this guarantee we show up in answers?

No. It makes the page worth quoting and tells crawlers what you want them to read. Being mentioned still depends on the engine.

Will a block hurt search?

A block aimed at a training crawler is a different switch from a search crawler. We name which is which before anything is blocked. If we cannot tell them apart, we do not block.

What is the plain-text note?

A file on the site that introduces the business and lists the pages worth reading. People can read it too. We do not hide claims there that are not on the site.

Do we need new pages?

Only if the current ones do not answer the question. We do not publish filler to chase a crawler.

Can you use another client's numbers on our site?

No. The pirx.ca figures stay attached to pirx.ca and to the date they were pulled. Your report will be yours.

How often do the figures move?

The number on this page is the Google Search Console window Jul 14 to Oct 13, 2026. We do not refresh it from memory.
