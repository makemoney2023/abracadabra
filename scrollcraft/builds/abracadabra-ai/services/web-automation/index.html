Web automation, scraping, PDFs and reports

What it is

A repeatable job that visits the sites or files you name, collects what you asked for, and turns it into a spreadsheet or a report. It runs on a schedule, or when you press go.

What it looks like in your business

  • Turning a weekly spreadsheet into a report
  • Checking a public page for a price, a status, or a change, and writing that down
  • Filling the same PDF or the same form from a row in a sheet

How it works

  1. You show us the job as you do it once, click by click.
  2. We turn that into a run that opens the pages, reads the fields, and writes the result.
  3. The run happens on Cloudflare, on the schedule you pick.
  4. If a page changes and the run fails, you get the failure, not a silent wrong number.

What you get

  • The automated run, on a schedule or a button
  • The output you asked for: a sheet, a PDF, or a report
  • A log of each run: what it opened, what it wrote, and whether it finished
  • An alert when a run fails

Who it's for

Owners who still do the same browser job every week by hand.

Signs you need it

  • Someone copies numbers from a page into a sheet on a fixed day
  • A report is late because the person who builds it is out
  • The steps are the same, and the only change is the date

Built on Browser Run, Workflows. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Can it log into a site that needs a password?

Only with an account you control, and only for the job you described. We do not share that login with a model provider.

What if the page layout changes?

The run stops and tells you. We adjust it. It does not invent the missing number.

Will it collect someone else's private data?

No. We automate pages and files you are allowed to use: your own accounts, public pages, and exports you already download.

How often can it run?

On the schedule you set: hourly, nightly, weekly, or when you start it.
