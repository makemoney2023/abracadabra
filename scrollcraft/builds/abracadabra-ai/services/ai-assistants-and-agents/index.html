The shift

Great answers. Then nothing happens.

Customers ask the same questions every day, often after you have moved on to something else. Each new message arrives without the history of who they are, what they asked, and which rule applies. You send a familiar paragraph, and the booking or the follow-up still has to happen in another system.

Most chatbots can talk but can’t do anything. Yours knows each customer, follows your rules, finishes the request, and brings in your staff when it should.

The outcome

What you get

  • Remembers every customer

    Each reply picks up where the last one left off, and every customer’s history stays private.

  • Requests that get done

    Bookings, status checks and callbacks are completed in the tools you already use.

  • A handoff with the context

    When the question sits outside your rules, a person receives the thread and the facts already gathered.

  • A clear record

    See what was done, why, and when a person took over.

Where it earns its keep

Use cases

  • After-hours questions

    The assistant answers the questions you have already written down. Everything else waits until morning, with the thread attached.

  • Status on an open request

    Customers get an instant update on their order or job without you digging through your inbox.

  • A routine request, completed

    A booking, a callback, or a form is completed in the system you already use. The customer is not asked to start the request again.

A small program, run safely

When a task needs a calculation or a file check, the assistant runs it in a locked-down space with strict limits. See how safe code works

The operating model

How it works

From first conversation to a system that runs itself.

  1. Plan

    We map how the work gets done today, including the calls only a person should make, and agree on it before anything is built.

  2. Build

    Built on Cloudflare and connected to the tools you already use, so nothing has to move.

  3. Review

    Tested on your real work from last week before any customer sees it.

  4. Launch and run

    We launch with close monitoring, keep it running, and you can change the rules at any time. It’s yours to keep.

Questions, answered

FAQ

faq-title

Will it invent an answer?

It answers from the rules and the records you give it. When those do not cover the question, it says so and hands the conversation to a person.

Can it take actions, or only chat?

Both, within the jobs on the list. A booking or a status update is an action. Anything outside that list waits for a person.

Does it remember an earlier conversation?

Yes, for the customer or the thread that is connected. A new person does not see someone else’s history.

What has to be maintained?

The rules and the facts. When a policy or a price changes, you update that source, and the assistant follows the new version.

Can it email a customer on its own?

Only for the messages on the list, and only after you have reviewed that step. It never sends a message you haven’t approved.
