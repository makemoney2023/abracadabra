AI assistants and agents that remember customers and take actions

What it is

An assistant your customers or your team can talk to. It keeps the thread, follows the rules you set, and can do a small job when the answer is clear: book a time, send a follow-up, or hand the conversation to a person.

What it looks like in your business

  • Answering common customer questions after hours, and handing off when the question needs a person
  • Following up on an open request with the status you already have on file
  • Taking a routine request, such as a booking, a callback, or a form, and filing it where your team already works

How it works

  1. We write down the jobs the assistant is allowed to do, and the ones it must pass to a person.
  2. We connect it to the records it needs, so it is not guessing from a blank page.
  3. It runs on Cloudflare, so the conversation and the memory stay with the request.
  4. You try it on real questions from last week. We tighten the rules before it talks to customers.

What you get

  • The assistant, live, with the jobs it may do written down
  • A memory of the conversation so the next reply does not start over
  • A handoff to a person when the request is outside those jobs
  • A short record of what it did

Who it's for

Owners who are tired of the same questions landing in the same inbox, and who want a first reply that follows the way the business already works.

Signs you need it

  • The same questions arrive every day, and the answer is already known
  • Customers wait because nobody is watching the inbox after hours
  • Staff retype the same details into a second tool

Built on Agents SDK, Durable Objects, Agent Memory, Workflows. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Will it invent an answer?

It answers from the rules and the records you give it. When it does not know, it says so and hands the conversation to a person.

Can it take actions, or only chat?

Both, within the jobs you approve. A booking or a status update is an action. Anything outside that list waits for a person.

Does it remember an earlier conversation?

Yes, for the customer or the thread you connect. A new person does not see someone else's history.

What do we have to maintain?

The rules and the facts. When a policy or a price changes, you update that source. The assistant follows the new version.
