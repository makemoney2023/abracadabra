The conversation ends before the work begins

Customers ask the same questions every day, often after you have moved on to something else. Each new message arrives without the history of who they are, what they asked, and which rule applies. You send a familiar paragraph, and the booking or the follow-up still has to happen in another system. The conversation and the work never meet.

A chat window can talk. It does not know this customer, and it cannot finish the request in the system you already use. When it guesses, the customer is the one who discovers the mistake. The assistant that belongs here answers from your rules, completes the request, and brings a person in when the question leaves that ground.

What you get

  • A conversation that continues

    The next reply already knows what this customer said. Another person's history stays out of the thread.

  • A request that is finished

    A booking, a status, or a callback is completed in the system you already use. Anything else waits for a person.

  • A handoff with the context

    When the question sits outside your rules, a person receives the thread and the facts already gathered.

  • A record of the work

    The action, the rule it followed, and the moment it stopped for a person remain visible afterward.

Use cases

  • After-hours questions

    The assistant answers the questions you have already written down. Everything else waits until morning, with the thread attached.

  • Status on an open request

    It reads the record you already keep and says where the work stands, so you are not hunting a second inbox for the same fact.

  • A routine request, completed

    A booking, a callback, or a form is completed in the system you already use. The customer is not asked to start the request again.

How it works

You tell us the job. We plan it, build it, and keep it running.

  1. Plan

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moments that still belong to a person. We write that down before anything is built, so the build has one picture of how you work.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect the records you already keep, so the work continues in the tools you already use.

  3. Review

    We test it on last week's real work. A customer-facing step goes live only after that test.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live with the first runs watched. You can change the rules, and you leave with the system.

FAQ

Will it invent an answer?

It answers from the rules and the records you give it. When those do not cover the question, it says so and hands the conversation to a person.

Can it take actions, or only chat?

Both, within the jobs on the list. A booking or a status update is an action. Anything outside that list waits for a person.

Does it remember an earlier conversation?

Yes, for the customer or the thread that is connected. A new person does not see someone else's history.

What has to be maintained?

The rules and the facts. When a policy or a price changes, you update that source, and the assistant follows the new version.

Can it email a customer on its own?

Only for the messages on the list, and only after you have reviewed that step. A surprise message is a failed design.
