The shift

Content that starts with your brand, not a blank prompt.

Pages go out without visuals, and recordings sit untranscribed because nobody has time.

A generated picture that ignores your material is worse than no picture: the wrong claim, the wrong label, a face you do not have the right to use. Fast is only useful if it’s right, so every file gets a human check.

The outcome

What you get

  • A draft from your inputs

    The image, the transcript, the clip, or the voice starts from the file, the footage, or the description you supplied.

  • Words a person can check

    Short labels are read by a person before they are public. Important wording is added as real text, so it’s always spelled right.

  • Your approval, every time

    Nothing is posted, mailed, or placed on a page until the person you named for that file has cleared it.

  • The file, ready to use

    The cleared asset is stored with a record of which draft was chosen, in a format the site or the post can use.

Where it earns its keep

Use cases

  • A picture for a page about to publish

    Describe the shot you need and pick your favourite from a set of drafts.

  • A transcript of a recording

    The recording stays the source. The transcript is a reading copy a person can correct before anyone quotes it.

  • A short clip from footage you already shot

    Cut from footage you’ve already shot, with your message on it.

The operating model

How it works

From first conversation to a system that runs itself.

  1. Plan

    We map how the work gets done today, including the calls only a person should make, and agree on it before anything is built.

  2. Build

    Built on Cloudflare and connected to the tools you already use, so nothing has to move.

  3. Review

    Tested on your real work from last week before any customer sees it.

  4. Launch and run

    We launch with close monitoring, keep it running, and you can change the rules at any time. It’s yours to keep.

Questions, answered

FAQ

faq-title

Will a picture of a customer be published?

No, unless you supply that photo and the right to use it. Drafts start from the description, the file, or the footage you provided.

Are the files yours to use?

You receive the file made for the use that was agreed. It is not resold, and it is not dropped into someone else’s work.

Can the text inside an image be trusted?

No, not by default. A person checks short labels. If a line must be exact, it is set as text on the page.

Do transcripts replace the recording?

They are a reading copy. If the words matter, the recording stays the source and the transcript is corrected against it.

Can it match a face from a photo found online?

No. A person’s likeness is not built from a picture you do not have rights to.

What if the whole set is wrong?

Then nothing ships. We make a new set.
