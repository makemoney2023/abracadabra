The shift

Your data knows more than your spreadsheet shows.

You have years of records. Orders, jobs, visits, readings. Somewhere in there is the answer to a question you keep asking. What happens next? How much? How soon? A spreadsheet can add it up. It can’t learn from it.

Many AI tools hand you a number with no reason. That is hard to trust, and harder to explain to a customer. We build models that learn from your own data, show their working, and say how sure they are. When the data is thin, they say so.

The outcome

What you get

  • A number you can use

    One clear answer to the question you ask most, worked out from your own records.

  • A fair range

    Every number comes with a range. When the data is thin or messy, the range gets wider instead of the number pretending.

  • The reasons, in plain words

    The number is split into the parts that moved it, so anyone can see why it changed.

  • Steady, not jumpy

    One odd day does not swing the result. The number moves when something real has changed.

Where it earns its keep

Use cases

  • What a customer can expect

    Turn a customer’s own history into a clear estimate they can plan around, the way PIRX gives a runner a Projected Time.

  • What is coming next

    Spot the pattern in past jobs or orders, so you can plan people and stock before the busy weeks.

  • Which ones need a look

    Flag the records that look off, with the reason, so a person checks those first.

The model lives inside the app

The model is part of the product, not a report on the side. Your team and your customers see the number, the range and the reasons where they already work. We build the app around it too. See how we build apps

The operating model

How it works

From first conversation to a system that runs itself.

  1. Plan

    We map how the work gets done today, including the calls only a person should make, and agree on it before anything is built.

  2. Build

    Built on Cloudflare and connected to the tools you already use, so nothing has to move.

  3. Review

    Tested on your real work from last week before any customer sees it.

  4. Launch and run

    We launch with close monitoring, keep it running, and you can change the rules at any time. It’s yours to keep.

Named work

Proof

pirx.ca

We designed and built the PIRX app and its machine learning. A runner connects the watch they already wear. PIRX gives a Projected Time for a race, a Supported Range around it, and the seconds each of five Structural Drivers adds: Aerobic Base, Threshold Density, Speed Exposure, Running Economy and Load Consistency. Personal models, including gradient boosting, learn each runner’s own patterns as their history grows. They are only used when they beat the app’s safe rules.

Questions, answered

FAQ

faq-title

How much data do we need?

Enough history to learn from. We look at what you have first. If it is too thin, we tell you, and we start with simple rules instead.

Will the model make things up?

It works only from your data. When the data is thin or odd, the range gets wider and the app says so.

Can we see why a number changed?

Yes. Each number is split into the parts that moved it, in plain words.

What if a model gets it wrong?

A new model is tested against safe rules first. If it does worse, or gives an odd answer, the app goes back to the rules.

Who owns the data and the model?

You do. The data stays yours, and so does what we build on it.
