Search-grounded research tools

What it is

A tool that answers a research question from current pages on the web, and shows the links it used. It is for questions your own documents do not cover.

What it looks like in your business

  • Checking a public claim against pages published this month
  • Pulling a short brief before a meeting, with the links underneath
  • Comparing two public sources side by side instead of trusting one summary

How it works

  1. You ask the question in plain language, and you say what a useful answer includes.
  2. The tool searches the web, reads the pages, and writes the brief with links.
  3. The search runs on Cloudflare. The links stay attached to the sentences they support.
  4. You open the links that matter. The brief is a start, not a source.

What you get

  • A research tool the team can ask
  • Answers with the links beside the claims
  • A way to save a brief you want to keep
  • A reminder, on the page, that the links are the source

Who it's for

Owners who need a current answer from the public web and do not want an unsourced paragraph.

Signs you need it

  • Someone spends an hour opening tabs before a decision
  • A pasted summary has no link, and nobody can tell if it is current
  • Your own files do not cover the question, because the facts live outside the company

Built on Web Search API. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

How is this different from asking a chatbot?

The answer has to point at pages. If it cannot find a page, it says so.

Can it read pages behind a login?

No. It reads public pages. Private files belong in the document search, not here.

Will it cite a page it did not open?

No. Every claim you are meant to trust carries a link, and you can open it.

Is the brief advice?

It is a reading list with a summary. Decisions stay with you.
