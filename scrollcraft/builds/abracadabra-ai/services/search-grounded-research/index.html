The shift

Big decisions deserve real sources.

An AI answer can sound right and still be out of date. Checking it by hand takes an hour you don’t have.

A chatbot will still answer without opening a page. Every answer here is backed by pages you can open.

The outcome

What you get

  • The links stay attached

    Every claim links to its source.

  • A refusal when the web is thin

    If the search does not support the answer, the reply says so. It does not smooth the gap into a paragraph.

  • Public sources only

    It reads what anyone can open online, and keeps your private files out of it.

  • An answer you can keep

    Save answers with their links for later.

Where it earns its keep

Use cases

  • A claim checked against this month

    The question is whether a public statement is still true. The reply shows the pages, including one that contradicts it if that page exists.

  • A short answer before a meeting

    The question is specific. The reply is short, with the links under it, so the room can open the page that matters.

  • Two public sources, side by side

    The question asks for the disagreement, not a blend. The reply keeps the two pages apart and says where they differ.

The operating model

How it works

From first conversation to a system that runs itself.

  1. Plan

    We map how the work gets done today, including the calls only a person should make, and agree on it before anything is built.

  2. Build

    Built on Cloudflare and connected to the tools you already use, so nothing has to move.

  3. Review

    Tested on your real work from last week before any customer sees it.

  4. Launch and run

    We launch with close monitoring, keep it running, and you can change the rules at any time. It’s yours to keep.

FAQ

How is this different from asking a chatbot?

The answer has to point at pages. If it cannot find a page, it says so.

Can it read pages behind a login?

No. It reads public pages. Private files belong in answers from your documents, not here.

Will it cite a page it did not open?

No. Every claim meant to be trusted carries a link you can open. A remembered citation is not good enough.

Is the answer advice?

It gives you the facts and sources. The decision is yours.

How current is it?

As current as the pages opened for that run. A saved answer is frozen on that day. Ask again if the week matters.

Can this replace someone who knows the field?

No. It shortens the reading. A person who knows the field still decides what the pages mean.
