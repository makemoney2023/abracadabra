A decision needs a source, not a paragraph

Someone pastes a paragraph before a decision. It sounds current. It has no link. The person who has to own the decision either trusts it or spends an hour opening tabs to see whether it is real. Trusting it is how a stale page becomes a fact inside your business. Rechecking it by hand is the job the tool was supposed to do.

A chatbot will still answer when it did not open a page. That is the wrong product for a question that has to be current. Your own documents are the wrong collection too, when the fact lives on the public web.

What you get

  • The links stay attached

    A claim meant to be trusted carries a page. You can open the page. A sentence with no page is not a finding.

  • A refusal when the web is thin

    If the search does not support the answer, the reply says so. It does not smooth the gap into a paragraph.

  • Public pages only

    It reads what a stranger could open. A page behind a login is out of scope, on purpose.

  • An answer you can keep

    You can save the reply worth keeping, and the saved copy still has the links. It is a start, not a source.

Use cases

  • A claim checked against this month

    The question is whether a public statement is still true. The reply shows the pages, including one that contradicts it if that page exists.

  • A short answer before a meeting

    The question is specific. The reply is short, with the links under it, so the room can open the page that matters.

  • Two public sources, side by side

    The question asks for the disagreement, not a blend. The reply keeps the two pages apart and says where they differ.

How it works

You tell us the job. We plan it, build it, and keep it running.

  1. Plan

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moments that still belong to a person. We write that down before anything is built, so the build has one picture of how you work.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect the records you already keep, so the work continues in the tools you already use.

  3. Review

    We test it on last week's real work. A customer-facing step goes live only after that test.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live with the first runs watched. You can change the rules, and you leave with the system.

FAQ

How is this different from asking a chatbot?

The answer has to point at pages. If it cannot find a page, it says so. Fluency is not a source.

Can it read pages behind a login?

No. It reads public pages. Private files belong in answers from your documents, not here.

Will it cite a page it did not open?

No. Every claim meant to be trusted carries a link you can open. A remembered citation is not good enough.

Is the answer advice?

It is a reading list with a summary. The decision stays with you. A source list is not dressed up as a recommendation to buy, sue, or treat.

How current is it?

As current as the pages opened for that run. A saved answer is frozen on that day. Ask again if the week matters.

Can this replace someone who knows the field?

No. It shortens the reading. A person who knows the field still decides what the pages mean.
