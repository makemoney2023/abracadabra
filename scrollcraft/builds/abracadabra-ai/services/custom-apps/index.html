Custom apps and back offices

What it is

An app built around the way your team already works: the screens, the order, the handoff. It replaces the pile of sheets and inboxes that grew up around that work.

What it looks like in your business

  • A back office where a request moves from intake to done, in the order your team already uses
  • A simple screen for the status your customers keep asking about
  • Replacing a weekly spreadsheet that three people edit and nobody quite trusts

How it works

  1. You walk us through a real Tuesday: who touches the work, in what order, and where it gets stuck.
  2. We build the smallest app that follows that order, and we leave out the features nobody asked for.
  3. It runs on Cloudflare, so the people who need it can open it without a machine in a back room.
  4. You use it on live work. We change the screen where the order was wrong.

What you get

  • The app, deployed, with the roles you named
  • The records it keeps, in place of the sheet
  • A short map of who can see what
  • A way to export the data if you leave

Who it's for

Owners whose real process does not fit a product off the shelf, and who are done bending the team around the software.

Signs you need it

  • The work lives in a spreadsheet, an inbox, and someone's memory
  • An off-the-shelf tool almost fits, and the gap is where mistakes happen
  • Two people cannot tell you the same status for the same job

Built on Workers, D1, R2. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Do we have to change how we work?

The screen follows the order you already trust. Where that order is the problem, we say so before we build it.

Can the current staff use it without a training course?

The first version is the path they already walk. If a screen needs a manual, it is not done.

Who owns the data?

You do. We can export it. We do not lock it to a format you cannot leave.

Is this a rebuild of every tool we use?

No. We replace the pile that is actually in the way. The tools that already fit can stay.
