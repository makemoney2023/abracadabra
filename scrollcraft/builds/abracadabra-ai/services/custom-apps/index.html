The application should follow the work

The rented app has the right nouns and the wrong order. People export, repair the row somewhere else, and import, or they keep the side record and let the app go stale. A new hire learns that side record, not the product you pay for. The record of the business is whichever file was saved last.

Buying another module adds a login. Hiring a developer without a written job adds a queue. The missing piece is usually small and specific: the estimate, the booking, the record you actually trust, the public page a buyer reads before they ask.

What you get

  • A screen in the real order

    The first thing you do in the morning is the first thing on the screen. Fields nobody uses are not there.

  • One record

    The customer, the job, and the number live in one place. A side record is a sign the application is not finished.

  • A public page when the job needs one

    If a buyer or a caller has to understand the offer before they talk to you, that page is part of the application, not a separate project that drifts.

  • A record you can leave with

    You can export the record. It is not kept in a format you cannot leave.

Use cases

  • The record you work in

    Customers, jobs, and the numbers, in the order the day actually happens. The week no longer ends in a reconciliation between two tools.

  • A request that becomes work

    An estimate, a booking, or a form on the public site lands in the record. It does not have to be entered again to become real.

  • A page a buyer can finish

    The offer, the comparison, and the questions are on the page. The buyer arrives having read them, and you are not repeating the same explanation.

How it works

You tell us the job. We plan it, build it, and keep it running.

  1. Plan

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moments that still belong to a person. We write that down before anything is built, so the build has one picture of how you work.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect the records you already keep, so the work continues in the tools you already use.

  3. Review

    We test it on last week's real work. A customer-facing step goes live only after that test.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live with the first runs watched. You can change the rules, and you leave with the system.

FAQ

Do the tools you already pay for have to go?

No. Only the pile that is actually in the way changes. A tool people already like can stay, and the plan says which is which.

Can the people who use it skip a course?

The first version is the path they already walk. A screen that needs a manual is not finished.

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export the record. It is not locked to a format you cannot leave.

How is the fit checked?

You run last week's real work through it before it becomes the system of record. A demo with sample rows is not that test.

Will every department be rebuilt?

No. One job, end to end. A second job is a second project.
