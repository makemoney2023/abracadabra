A voice line that answers from your facts and hands the call to a person

A caller should get the fact you already keep, in a voice that can pause and hand the call to a person. A script that cannot see your record just keeps them on the line.

The phone still depends on who is free

Calls arrive while the person who can answer is with someone else. A queue plays music. A missed call becomes a voicemail nobody trusts, or a callback that starts the conversation from zero. The caller repeats the account, the order, or the question.

A phone tree can route a button press. It cannot read the record and say where the job stands. A person still does that, if they pick up. After hours, they do not.

What changes

  • An answer from your facts

    The line says what the record says. It does not fill a silence with a plausible sentence.

  • A handoff you can hear

    When the call leaves the script, a person gets the line and a short note of what was already said.

  • A limit on what it may do

    It can book, confirm, or take a message if you approved that. It cannot change a record that is not on the list.

  • A recording of the run

    You can hear the call and read the note. A bad answer is a specific fix, not a feeling.

Three places it shows up

  • A question you already answer

    Hours, status, what to bring, where to send a file. The line reads the source you keep and says that, then offers a person if the caller needs more.

  • A booking or a callback

    The caller asks for a time or a call back. The request lands where the team already works, and the caller is not asked to leave the same details in a voicemail.

  • After the office is closed

    The line stays up. It answers the closed-hours list and takes the rest as a message a person sees in the morning, with the call attached.

How it gets built

The order is the same on every service. The job is what changes.

  1. Brief

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moment a person has to say yes. We write that list before anyone builds. A step that is not on the list does not ship.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect it to the records you already keep. The new system should not be a second place to type the same fact.

  3. People approve

    Your team runs last week's real work through it and marks what may go live. We do not put a customer-facing step in front of people on our say-so alone.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live. We watch the first runs with you, fix what the real work shows, and leave the rules where your team can change them.

Questions

Will it sound like a person we employ?

It sounds like the voice you pick, and it says only what the record supports. We do not imitate a named staff member.

Can it transfer to a real phone?

Yes, to the number you set, with a short note of the call so far. If nobody picks up, it takes a message.

What about a noisy line or a strong accent?

If it did not hear the question, it asks again. It does not guess a name or a number it did not catch.

Are calls stored?

The calls you choose to keep, for the reason you write down. You can turn retention off. We do not keep a private copy for ourselves.

Can it take a payment on the call?

Only if that action is on the approved list and the payment path is one you already use. Otherwise it books a person.

Does this replace the team on the phone?

It takes the calls you listed. The calls that need judgment still go to a person. If your volume does not justify that split, we will say so.

Play us one call from last week that should have been answered from the record. We will mark the sentence the line may say, and the sentence that must be a person.
