Voice and real-time AI agents

What it is

A phone or voice conversation that answers in the moment. It listens, replies from the facts you provide, and passes the call to a person when the request needs one.

What it looks like in your business

  • Answering common customer questions after hours, by voice
  • Taking a name, a number, and the reason for the call, then filing that note
  • Transferring the call when the caller asks for a person, or when the question is outside the script

How it works

  1. We write the calls it may handle, and the line it uses to reach a person.
  2. We connect the facts it is allowed to say.
  3. The voice runs on Cloudflare, so the reply stays in the same moment as the caller.
  4. You listen to real calls from a quiet test line before it answers the public number.

What you get

  • A voice agent on the number or the line you choose
  • A short script of what it may say and what it must not
  • A transfer to a person
  • A note of each call: what was asked, and what it did

Who it's for

Owners whose phone still rings with questions a person repeats all day.

Signs you need it

  • After-hours callers leave a message and call again in the morning
  • Staff spend the first hour returning the same kinds of calls
  • People hang up when they are put on hold

Built on Realtime, Workers AI. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Will it sound like a person from our team?

It sounds like a clear voice reading the facts you approved. It does not pretend to be a named employee.

What if it mishears?

It confirms the detail that matters, such as a name or a number, before it files anything.

Can it call out, or only answer?

We start with answering. Outbound calls are a separate decision, with the list of who may be called.

Where do the recordings live?

With the call record you asked us to keep, for as long as you set. We do not keep a recording you did not ask for.
