Every new model arrives as another account

A team starts with one model. Then a draft needs a different one, an image needs another, and a long document needs a third. Each vendor has its own key, its own invoice, and its own way of failing. Nobody can say what last month cost, or which job caused it.

Switching gets postponed because the switch is a project. The team keeps using the model they already wired, even when it is the expensive one for a simple job or the weak one for a careful answer.

What changes

  • One gateway

    The models you approve are called through one place. Adding one does not mean a new vendor login for the whole team.

  • The model matches the job

    A short draft, a careful answer, and an image do not have to share a single model. You pick, and you can change the pick.

  • A bill you can stop

    Usage sits on one bill. If a model is not earning its place, you turn it off without unwiring the rest.

  • No invented spend story

    We do not publish a client's invoice. The proof on this page is the gateway itself, the providers named on the homepage.

Three places it shows up

  • A draft, then a check

    A fast model writes the first pass. A second model, or a person, checks the claims. You are not locked to the writer for the review.

  • An image or a transcript beside the text

    The job needs a different provider than the one that writes paragraphs. It still lands in the same system and on the same bill.

  • Turning a model off

    A provider changes price or quality. You stop sending it work. The jobs that remain keep running on the models you left in place.

How it gets built

The order is the same on every service. The job is what changes.

  1. Brief

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moment a person has to say yes. We write that list before anyone builds. A step that is not on the list does not ship.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect it to the records you already keep. The new system should not be a second place to type the same fact.

  3. People approve

    Your team runs last week's real work through it and marks what may go live. We do not put a customer-facing step in front of people on our say-so alone.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live. We watch the first runs with you, fix what the real work shows, and leave the rules where your team can change them.

Questions

Do we have to use every model on the list?

No. The list is what we can route. You approve the ones this business may call. The others stay off.

Is this a discount on model prices?

No. You pay for the models you use, through one bill. We do not advertise a rate, and we do not promise a cheaper token.

Where do the prompts and files go?

Through the gateway, on Cloudflare. We name the provider that actually runs the model. A prompt is not quietly copied to a second place.

Can we keep a model we already pay for?

If it is on the gateway, yes. You do not have to throw away a working account to start.

What if a provider has an outage?

The job can fall back to another model you already approved, or it can wait. We write that choice in the brief. We do not silently swap in a model you never allowed.

Who holds the keys?

The gateway holds the provider keys. Your team does not paste them into personal accounts. Removing a person does not mean hunting for keys.
