Any model, one controlled bill

What it is

Different jobs want different models. This puts them behind one door: you pick the model per job, you see the spend in one place, and you can stop a runaway bill.

What it looks like in your business

  • Sending everyday questions to a smaller model and harder questions to a stronger one
  • Capping what a busy week can spend before the invoice surprises you
  • Switching models when one is slow, without rebuilding the product

How it works

  1. We list the jobs and which model should handle each one.
  2. Traffic goes through one gateway on Cloudflare, so every call is logged the same way.
  3. You set a limit. When the limit is close, the system slows or stops instead of spending quietly.
  4. You read one usage view, not a pile of provider dashboards.

What you get

  • One gateway in front of the models you choose
  • A per-job choice of model
  • A spend limit you can change
  • A usage record you can read

Who it's for

Owners who want the right model for the job and do not want five invoices or a surprise bill.

Signs you need it

  • You are paying more than one model vendor and cannot see the total
  • A test or a loop spent more than you meant to
  • You want to try a new model without a rebuild

Built on AI Gateway, Workers AI. Built and deployed on Cloudflare's global network.

Questions

Do we have to pick one model forever?

No. The gateway is the stable part. The model behind a job can change.

Can we set a hard stop on spend?

Yes. You set the limit. Past that, calls stop or fall back to a cheaper path you approved.

Does this train a model on our data?

We do not turn your requests into training data. The providers you choose have their own terms, and we pick the setting that keeps your prompts out of training when that setting exists.

Is this the product our customers see?

Usually no. It is the control behind the product: which model runs, what it costs, and when it stops.
