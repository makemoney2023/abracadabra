A second model should not mean a second account

The second model arrives because the first one is wrong for part of the work. It also arrives as a new login, a new key, and a new invoice written in that vendor's words. Someone on your side becomes the person who knows where the keys live. When they leave, the map leaves with them.

A single model for every job is the other failure. The draft is slow, the careful answer is sloppy, or the image comes from a product that was never meant to write. The useful shape is a gateway: the model matches the job, the usage sits on one bill, and a model that is not earning its place can be turned off.

What you get

  • One gateway

    The models you use are called through one place. Adding one does not mean a new vendor login for everyone.

  • The model matches the job

    A short draft, a careful answer, and an image do not have to share a single model. You can change the choice.

  • A bill you can stop

    Usage sits on one bill. If a model is not earning its place, you turn it off without unwiring the rest of the work.

  • No invented spend story

    We do not publish anyone's invoice here. The proof is the gateway itself, and the providers named on the homepage.

Use cases

  • A draft, then a check

    A fast model writes the first pass. A second model, or a person, checks the claims. The review is not locked to the writer.

  • An image or a transcript beside the text

    The job needs a different provider than the one that writes paragraphs. It still lands in the same system and on the same bill.

  • Turning a model off

    A provider changes price or quality. You stop sending that model work. The jobs that remain keep running on the models you left in place.

How it works

You tell us the job. We plan it, build it, and keep it running.

  1. Plan

    You tell us the job, the rules, and the moments that still belong to a person. We write that down before anything is built, so the build has one picture of how you work.

  2. Build

    We build it on Cloudflare and connect the records you already keep, so the work continues in the tools you already use.

  3. Review

    We test it on last week's real work. A customer-facing step goes live only after that test.

  4. Launch and run

    It goes live with the first runs watched. You can change the rules, and you leave with the system.

FAQ

Does every model on the list have to be used?

No. The list is what can be routed. You choose which models your business may call. The others stay off.

Is this a discount on model prices?

No. The models you use are paid for through one bill. A rate is not advertised here, and a cheaper token is not promised.

Where do the prompts and files go?

Through the gateway, on Cloudflare. The provider that actually runs the model is named. A prompt is not quietly copied onward.

Can a model you already pay for stay?

If it is on the gateway, yes. A working account does not have to be thrown away to start.

What if a provider has an outage?

The job can fall back to another model already on the list, or it can wait. You write that choice down. A model you never allowed is not swapped in quietly.

Who holds the keys?

The gateway holds the provider keys. They are not pasted into personal accounts. Removing a person does not mean hunting for keys.
