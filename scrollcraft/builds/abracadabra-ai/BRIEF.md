## Interview (self-authored)

1. **Vibe in three to five words, plus references.**  
   Quiet conjure. Museum of working software. Physical proof, not pitch.  
   References: a night-closed auction house; a typesetter’s job bag; the Showdesk promo itself (real product in device chrome, not a generated world).

2. **The scroll journey, section by section.**  
   Walk into a room that already holds the first piece of work. Read its label. Pass Schema, the course, the appliance store. A quiet empty plinth. Then the hand scrubs the Showdesk film. Sales enablement as the last specimen. The ask is typed like every other label.

3. **The energy curve.**  
   Calm at the door. Steady through the room. Near-silent before the film. Intense only while the wheel scrubs that one clip. Calm again at the inquiry plate.

4. **How should someone feel, and the one moment they remember.**  
   Curiosity, then recognition that these are live products, then appetite for the set, then quiet, then awe at the scrub, then confidence, then resolve.  
   The remembered moment: “I turned the wheel and their Showdesk film played under my hand.”

5. **One thing no site they have seen does.**  
   The word ABRACADABRA is five syllables on a fixed utterance strip. Each specimen passed inks one syllable. The page is finished when the word is complete.

6. **How far from premium-minimal.**  
   Editorial catalog, not brutalist, not maximalist. Dark room, one red stamp, museum labels. Closer to a print catalog than to a SaaS landing page.

7. **One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?**  
   Two pages, two answers. The catalog (`index.html`) is distinct scenes: four products and an enablement pack are separate objects in one room. The walkthrough (`walkthrough.html`) is one unbroken world: eight legs of real product footage chained as a single scroll-scrubbed camera flight on the scroll-world engine. The first draft answered "distinct scenes" for both and shipped the walkthroughs as a screening-room grid of separate players; that was the wrong call for the walkthrough and was corrected.

8. **What assets do they already have?**  
   Showdesk composed MP4s and posters (copied from the public repo). Live Playwright recordings of Showdesk, Schema, LLMCourse, and CDA. Sales-enablement one-pagers and demo scripts written in this repo. No brand kit, no logo file.
