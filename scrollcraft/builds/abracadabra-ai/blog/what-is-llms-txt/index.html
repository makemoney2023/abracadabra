What Is llms.txt, and Who Actually Reads It

A proposed file that points models at your best pages. Google says it does not use the file. A large study found most copies are never requested.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

llms.txt is a proposed text file at the root of a site. Jeremy Howard published the proposal in September 2024, and the page was updated in August 2026.1 The idea is a short list of the pages you want a model to read. This site already publishes its own file. That is a fact about this site, not a traffic statistic.

Google's guide says Google does not use llms.txt for generative features in search.2

The missing-file guide is the check. The answer-engine note is what Google does require. The GEO comparison is the research.

What a crawl of the files found

Ahrefs, in a study published in June 2026, looked at 137,210 domains in its web analytics that had traffic in May 2026.3 28% published an llms.txt file, about 38,000 valid files. 97% of those files received zero requests in May. 96% of the requests that did arrive were bots. 19.5% of requests came from named AI tools, and 12% from audit or scanner tools.
