How to Use AI in Your Business: Twelve Jobs

Pick a job you already do, write the steps, and keep a person on the output. A Canadian survey shows which uses, and which follow-on work, are common.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

How to use AI in a business is a job list, not a model list. Statistics Canada's 2025 brief asked businesses that already use AI which tools they use and what they changed.1 Among those businesses, text analytics accounted for 35.7%, and marketing automation for 23.1%. 40.1% had developed new workflows, and 38.9% had trained staff. In the same period, 12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% a year earlier.

The help note separates that production question from generative-AI use. The automation note is how to pick the first job. The agents note is when the steps branch.

Twelve jobs you can write down

Draft the customer reply, then a person sends it. Summarize the meeting, then a person accepts the tasks. Turn notes into a quote, then a person sets the price. Sort the inbox, then a person decides what is answered. Flag the late invoice, then a person decides the chase. Describe the product, then a person approves the page. Group support tickets, then a person picks the answer. Check the inventory exception, then a person orders. Rewrite the policy in plain language, then a person publishes it. Compare two supplier notes, then a person chooses. Draft the weekly report, then a person accepts the numbers. Prepare the staff answer, then a person trains the team.

Questions

Which uses show up among Canadian businesses that use AI?

In the 2025 Statistics Canada brief, 35.7% of those businesses used text analytics and 23.1% used marketing automation.

What did those businesses change after they started?

40.1% developed new workflows and 38.9% trained staff. Those shares are among AI users, not among all firms.

What share used AI to produce the goods or the service?

12.2% of businesses, up from 6.1% a year earlier. That is a different question from generative-AI use.
