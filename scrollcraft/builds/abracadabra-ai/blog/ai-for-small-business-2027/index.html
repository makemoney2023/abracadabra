AI for Small Business: What the Surveys Still Say

Two surveys still disagree because they ask different questions. One counts generative AI use. The other counts AI used to produce the goods or the service.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

The useful question for a small business is still which job changes. Two 2025 surveys remain the clean public counts, and they do not measure the same thing.

The help note is the full split. The strategy note is what to do with the gap. The assessment is how to score one firm.

Two questions, two answers

The Chamber's 2025 report says 58% of small businesses use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.1

Statistics Canada, for 2025, found 12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% a year earlier.2

Questions

Why do the two surveys disagree?

They ask different questions. The Chamber counts generative AI use. Statistics Canada counts AI used to produce goods or deliver services.

What did the Chamber report?

In the 2025 report, 58% of small businesses said they use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.

What did Statistics Canada report?

12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services in 2025, up from 6.1% a year earlier.
