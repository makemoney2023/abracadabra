How Can AI Help a Small Business? What Surveys Count

Two 2025 surveys count different work. This note separates generative-AI use from AI in production, then names the jobs owners hand over.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-22

Scheduled for 2026-10-22. This preview is not in the public index yet.

Two surveys from 2025 count AI in small firms, and they count different work. The U.S. Chamber asked who uses generative AI. Statistics Canada asked who used AI to produce goods or deliver services. This note keeps those claims apart, then names the jobs owners hand over. The pressure guide is the matching question for one business. Christopher Buzny writes these notes from the studio site.

Generative use is not production use

The Chamber's Empowering Small Business report says 58% of small businesses self-identified that they use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.1 A separate line in the same report says 58% of small businesses use four or more tech platforms. Those are two different facts. The first is about generative AI. The second is about how many platforms a firm already runs.

Statistics Canada, in the Canadian Survey on Business Conditions for the second quarter of 2025, found that 12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% in the second quarter of 2024.2 The release is dated 16 June 2025. Collection ran from 1 April to 5 May 2025. The agency received 21,357 responses, of which 9,103 came from businesses that used AI.

Among businesses that used AI, text analytics accounted for 35.7%, and marketing automation for 23.1%. 40.1% had developed new workflows, and 38.9% had trained staff.

Jobs an owner can hand over

The Chamber figure describes a tool someone opens. The StatCan figure describes AI inside the delivery of the product or the service. A small business can do the first for a long time without the second.

The jobs that show up in the StatCan breakdown are the practical list: reading and sorting text, sending and measuring marketing, writing a workflow that did not exist, and training the people who will run it. None of those is a promise that the tool will run the company.

The readiness note asks a different question: whether this firm, not the average firm, has the data, the process, the people, and the decision in place.

Questions

Why do small-business AI surveys disagree?

The Chamber counts generative AI use. Statistics Canada counts AI used to produce goods or deliver services. A firm can say yes to the first and no to the second.

What share of U.S. small businesses said they use generative AI?

In the 2025 Chamber report, 58% said they use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.

What share of Canadian businesses used AI in production?

In the second quarter of 2025, 12.2% used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% a year earlier.

What jobs show up among Canadian businesses that use AI?

Among AI users, 35.7% used text analytics and 23.1% used marketing automation. 40.1% developed new workflows and 38.9% trained staff.
