AI Consultant for Small Business: What to Ask

A useful consultant names the job, the data, and the stop. Most firms use AI somewhere. Far fewer are scaling agents. Guaranteed rankings are a red flag.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

An AI consultant for a small business should be able to say which job changes, which data it needs, and who can stop the work. If an off-the-shelf product already does that job, the honest answer is to buy the product. The studio works that way. The agency comparison is the hiring split. The stall note is what happens when the proof never becomes a process.

What gets canceled

Gartner said in June 2025 that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, citing cost, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls.1 In a poll of 3,412 respondents, 19% said their organization had made significant investments in agentic AI, 42% had made conservative investments, 8% had made none, and 31% were waiting or unsure. Gartner also said only about 130 vendors offered what it considered real agentic products.

McKinsey's 2025 survey puts the same gap in different numbers. 88% report regular AI use in at least one function, up from 78% a year earlier.2 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are experimenting. A consultant who quotes only the first figure is describing use, not a finished system.

Run the Readiness Check

Red flags are specific. Guaranteed rankings ignore Google's guide, which says generative features are still SEO.3 No data plan means the source of truth was never named. No handover means the business cannot run the thing after the invoice.

Questions

What should you ask an AI consultant first?

What job will change, where the data lives, who checks the output, and how you will know it failed. A pitch that starts with a tool name has skipped the work.

How often do agentic projects get canceled?

Gartner said in June 2025 that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, for cost, unclear value, or inadequate risk controls.

Is a promise of guaranteed rankings a red flag?

Yes. Google's own guide says generative features still depend on ordinary SEO. Nobody can guarantee a ranking in an overview.
