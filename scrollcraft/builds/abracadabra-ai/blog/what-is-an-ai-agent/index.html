What Is an AI Agent? A Plain Answer for Owners

An agent picks its own next step. A workflow follows a path you wrote. This note uses Anthropic's distinction and McKinsey's survey of who is scaling.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

An AI agent is not a chatbot with a longer prompt. Anthropic's December 2024 essay draws the line this way: a workflow runs large language models and tools on a path the builder defined, and an agent lets the model direct its own process and its own tool use.1 The essay also says to start with the simplest solution, and to add complexity only when the simpler system fails.

The process guide is the matching check when the steps are still in someone's head. The use-case note lists jobs. The comparison note stays on the vocabulary.

When a workflow is enough

Use a workflow when the steps are known: take the email, extract the fields, write the draft, wait for a person. Use an agent when the next step depends on what the last step found, and you cannot write every branch in advance. If you cannot name the stop condition, you do not have either one yet.

Kind of system Who picks the next step When it fits
One prompt The person at the keyboard A single draft or a single answer
Workflow The path you wrote The steps rarely change
Agent The model, inside bounds you set The path depends on what it finds

What the survey actually counts

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 88% of respondents report regular AI use in at least one business function, compared with 78% a year earlier.2 23% say their organizations are scaling an agentic system somewhere in the enterprise. An additional 39% are experimenting with agents. In any one function, no more than 10% say they are scaling agents. Firms under $100 million in revenue are less often at the scaling stage: 29%, against nearly half of firms over $5 billion.

Questions

What is an AI agent in plain language?

Anthropic describes an agent as a system where the model directs its own process and tool use. A workflow, by contrast, follows a path the builder wrote in advance.

Should a small business start with an agent?

Anthropic's December 2024 essay says to start with the simplest solution that works. A fixed workflow is enough when the steps do not change.

How many firms are scaling agents?

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 23% of respondents are scaling an agentic system somewhere in the enterprise, and 39% are experimenting. In any one function, no more than 10% say they are scaling agents.
