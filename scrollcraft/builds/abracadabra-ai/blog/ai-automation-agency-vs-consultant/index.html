AI Automation Agency vs Consultant: Who to Hire

Hire a consultant to name the job. Hire an agency to build after the job is named. Training plans and scaling rates are both thinner than use.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

An AI consultant names the job, the data, and the stop. An automation agency builds the system after that naming is done. Hiring the agency first produces a build with no owner. The consultant note is the question list. The buy-or-build note is whether to build at all. The decision guide is the matching score.

Two training figures, two populations

Microsoft's 2024 index says only 25% of companies were planning to offer generative AI training.1 Statistics Canada's 2025 brief says 38.9% of Canadian businesses that already use AI had trained staff.2 Those are different populations. One is a plan among companies. The other is training among businesses that already use AI. Do not average them.

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are experimenting.3 An agency pitch that quotes only a use figure is selling the experiment.

Questions

When do you hire a consultant instead of an agency?

When the job, the data, and the checker are not yet named. An agency build before that naming has nothing to implement.

Are the training figures the same fact?

No. Microsoft's 25% is companies planning generative AI training in 2024. Statistics Canada's 38.9% is AI-using Canadian businesses that had trained staff.

What does scaling look like in the survey?

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are still experimenting.
