AI-Ready Data: What a Small Business Needs First

AI-ready data means one current source for the job, readable by a system. Gartner's figures describe the firms that do not have that yet.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

AI-ready data, for a small business, is not a warehouse. It is one current source for the job you want a tool to do, in a form a system can read, with a person who knows which fields lie. If the answer lives only in someone's head, the tool will invent a second version of it.

The low-data guide and the mid-data guide are the matching fixes. The checklist asks the same question before a purchase.

What the research counts

Gartner reported in February 2025 that 63% of respondents do not have, or are unsure they have, the data management practices AI needs.1 Through 2026, the same note says, organizations will abandon 60% of AI projects unsupported by AI-ready data. The page's sample size is not cited here, because the article and its structured data do not agree.

A later Gartner article, from January 2026, says that by the end of last year at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept.2 The reasons it names are poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs, and unclear business value.

Questions

What is AI-ready data for a small business?

One current source for the job you want done, in a form a system can read, with someone who knows which fields are wrong. A data lake is not the requirement.

What share of leaders lack AI-ready data practices?

Gartner's February 2025 note says 63% do not have them, or are unsure they have them. Through 2026 it expects firms to abandon 60% of AI projects that lack AI-ready data.

Why do generative projects get abandoned?

Gartner's January 2026 article says at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept by the end of last year, for poor data quality, weak risk controls, rising cost, or unclear value.
