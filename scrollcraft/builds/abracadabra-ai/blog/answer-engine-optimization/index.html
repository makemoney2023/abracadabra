Answer Engine Optimization: What Google Still Requires

Google still treats answer-engine work as ordinary SEO. A research paper reports visibility gains. Pew found fewer clicks when a summary appears.

By Christopher Buzny · 2026-10-14

Answer engine optimization is the work of being cited when a generative answer is written. Google's guide says that work is still SEO.1 The page has to be indexed, and it has to be eligible for a snippet. Google says it does not use llms.txt, and it says there is no special chunking requirement. No extra schema is required for those features.

The visibility guide is the matching check. The ChatGPT note covers crawler files. The GEO comparison is the research split.

What the research measured

A 2024 paper on generative engine optimization reports visibility gains of up to 40%.2 Methods that add citations, quotations, and statistics were associated with gains over 40%. On Perplexity the reported gain reached 37%. Position-adjusted word count rose 41%, and a subjective impression score rose 28%. The benchmark used 10,000 queries.
