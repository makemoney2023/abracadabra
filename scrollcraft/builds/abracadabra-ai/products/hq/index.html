How work flows

From request to calendar, one path.

Digital workers do the building. People check it. You decide what goes out.

  1. 01

    Request

    Send a request by email, through the start-a-project form, or as a note on a piece in your space. HQ logs it and puts it in front of Client Services.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A card opens on your project board, tied to the request it came from, with a plan for what each piece of work must make.

  3. 03

    Agents build

    Each digital worker takes a piece, picks the skills it needs for the task, and builds. It files the work to HQ, where the work is held.

  4. 04

    Review

    Client Services checks the work against your request, and a person on our team signs off. If something is off, it goes back to the agent with a note.

  5. 05

    You approve

    Once the work is released to your space, you open a piece and approve it, ask for a change, or remove it. A change request opens a new card.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Finished work sits in named folders in your space, like Blog or Social. Each file shows its type, and pictures show a small thumbnail.

  7. 07

    Social calendar

    Approved work can be scheduled to your connected social networks. Approving a piece takes the next open slot on your calendar.

Review and approval

Checked before it reaches you. Approved by you before it goes anywhere.

HQ holds finished work until people have checked it. You see it when it is ready, and you make the call.

  • Held by default. Work an agent files stays out of your folders, previews, shared links and search until it is released.
  • Client Services reviews first. It checks the work against your request, and sends it back to the agent with a note when something is off.
  • You make the call. Approve a piece, ask for a change, or remove it. Removed work can be put back.

Folders and file types

Named folders. Every file labelled.

Finished work lands in named folders in your space, like Blog, Social or Brand. Each file shows its type, such as image, PDF, document or spreadsheet, and pictures show a thumbnail, so you can tell what is what without opening everything.

Your Library shows finished work as a wall of pictures: articles, social posts, pictures, video, websites and documents. You can share a piece with a read-only view link.

Social, automated

Approved work goes onto your social calendar.

Automated social posting is live in HQ, since October 21, 2026. Each client has a Social page with a calendar. Connect your accounts through each network’s own sign-in, and approved finished work takes the next open slot.

  • Instagram
  • Facebook
  • LinkedIn

  • X

  • Threads

  • Approved work only. Only finished work you have approved can be scheduled or posted. Work that is still held cannot.
  • Nothing goes out on its own. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

Digital workers

A team of agents that get work done, like magic.

Behind every card is a digital worker: an AI agent set up for your business with a clear role. Each client has a lead agent, and some have helper agents too. They read the card, build the work and file it to HQ for review. Think of it as a bench of specialists you do not have to hire or manage.

Skills chosen for every task

Agents do not carry a fixed toolkit. For each card, an agent searches HQ’s skill library for what the task needs, such as copywriting, design or search. It loads the skills that fit, attaches the skill packs to the card, and follows them. When it files the work, it lists the skills it used and says whether each one helped, with a short note.

Questions

What owners ask about HQ.

What is HQ?

HQ is the client workspace where Abracadabra’s team and digital workers deliver your work. It holds your requests, your board, your finished work, your folders and your social calendar.

Does anything reach me or my social networks without approval?

No. Work an agent files is held until it has been reviewed and released. Only finished work you have approved can be scheduled or posted.

Which social networks does HQ post to?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, YouTube, Threads, Google Business and Bluesky. Your accounts connect through each network’s own sign-in.

What are digital workers?

AI agents we set up for your business, each with a clear role. They work from cards on your board, use skills picked for each task, and file their work for review.
