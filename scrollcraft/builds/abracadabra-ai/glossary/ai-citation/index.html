Glossary · Search and AEO/GEO

AI citation

An AI citation is a link or source name that an AI tool shows with its answer to back up what it says. Perplexity shows numbered links, and Google’s AI Overviews show links to supporting websites. Being cited is not the same as getting a click.

Also called: AI source citation

AI citation, explained

Answer tools such as Perplexity, ChatGPT search and Google’s AI features write a reply, then point to the pages they used. Perplexity says each answer includes numbered citations that link to the original sources. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode show links to supporting websites.

For a business, a citation means an AI tool used your page as a source. But a reader may get what they need from the answer and never click. Pew found that people clicked result links less often when Google showed an AI summary. So we track citations and clicks as two different things.

How does a page get cited by an AI tool?

The tool has to be able to reach and read the page. For Google, the page must be indexed and able to show with a snippet, and Google says there are no extra technical rules for AI Overviews or AI Mode. After that, a page that answers a real question in plain words gives any answer tool something worth quoting.

Is an AI citation the same as a click?

No. A citation shows your page as a source. A click is when someone opens it. A person can read the answer, see your name and move on without visiting.

Questions

What does it mean when an AI tool cites my site?

It means the tool used your page as a source for its answer and showed a link or your name next to it. It does not mean the reader opened your page.

Do I need special code to be cited in Google’s AI features?

No. Google says you do not need new machine-readable files, AI text files or special schema markup to appear in its AI features.

Where can I see clicks from Google’s AI features?

Google says clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode are counted in the Search Console Performance report, under the Web search type.
