AI and Search Glossary.

A

Agentic AI
Agentic AI is a broad label for AI systems that can work toward a goal with limited supervision. It usually means one or more AI agents that plan steps and use tools. Sometimes several agents work together.
AI agent
An AI agent is a program that uses an AI model to choose its own next step toward a goal, such as checking a calendar or drafting a text. It works inside limits that people set.
AI citation
An AI citation is a link or source name that an AI tool shows with its answer to back up what it says. Perplexity shows numbered links, and Google’s AI Overviews show links to supporting websites. Being cited is not the same as getting a click.
AI readiness
AI readiness is how prepared a business is to put an AI tool to work and get value from it. It depends on the business more than the tool. That means its data, the way work gets done, its people and who makes decisions.
AI visibility (AI search visibility)
AI visibility is how often a business shows up in answers that AI tools write, and how well. It might be named, quoted or linked. There is no standard way to measure it, so each tool and report counts it in its own way.
Answer engine
An answer engine is a search tool that replies to a question with a written answer, usually with links to its sources, instead of only a list of links. Perplexity calls itself one. Google’s AI Overviews and AI Mode work in a similar way.
Answer engine optimization (AEO)
Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of getting your pages used and cited when an AI tool answers a question directly. Google says it is still SEO.

B

Business process automation (BPA)
Business process automation (BPA) means using software to do the repeated steps of a business job, like sending a bill to be approved. The aim is less manual work, fewer errors and the same result each time. It can run on simple rules, workflows or AI.

C

Crawler (bot)
A crawler is a program that visits web pages on its own and reads them, usually for a search engine or an AI company. It is also called a robot or spider. Sites use a robots.txt file to tell crawlers which pages they may visit.
Custom software
Custom software is software built for one business or group of users, around how they already work. Off-the-shelf software is different: it is made for many buyers and sold as a ready product. Custom software makes sense when no ready product does the job well.

G

Generative AI
Generative AI is AI that creates new content, such as text, images, audio, video or software code, in response to a request. Large language models, which write text, are the best-known kind.
Generative engine optimization (GEO)
Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of shaping web pages so AI tools that write answers are more likely to show and cite them. Examples of these tools are Google’s AI Overviews and Perplexity. Google says this work is still SEO.
Gradient boosting
Gradient boosting is a machine learning method that builds one strong model out of many weak ones. The weak ones are usually small decision trees. Each new tree learns from the errors the model still makes. It is added in a small step to shrink them.

H

HQ
HQ is Abracadabra’s client workspace, where our team and a crew of digital workers get your work done. Each request becomes a card with a plan. Nothing reaches your folders or your social networks until it has been reviewed and you have approved it.

L

Large language model (LLM)
A large language model (LLM) is an AI model trained on a huge amount of text. It writes by predicting the next word, or part of a word, over and over. Google’s Gemini and OpenAI’s GPT models are examples.
llms.txt
llms.txt is a proposed file, written in Markdown, that gives AI tools a short summary of a website and links to its most useful pages. It usually sits at the site’s root, at /llms.txt. Google Search says it does not use the file.

M

Machine learning
Machine learning is a way to build software that learns patterns from past data. It does not rely only on rules a person wrote. The trained model can then make predictions about new data, such as next month’s orders.

R

Readiness Check
The Readiness Check is our online check for a business that wants to put AI to work. It takes five to seven minutes. It scores readiness, growth and website visibility, and names the stage your business is at. It gives you next steps for your business.

S

Schema markup and JSON-LD
Schema markup is code added to a web page that labels what the page is about, such as an article, a product or a business. The labels come from a shared list called schema.org. JSON-LD is the format Google recommends for it. It is a block of code that machines read and visitors do not see.

W

Workflow
A workflow is the set of steps that takes a piece of work from start to finish, in a set order. In AI, it also means a system where an AI model and tools follow steps written in code ahead of time. That is different from an AI agent, which picks its own next step.
