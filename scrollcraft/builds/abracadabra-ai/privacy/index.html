Privacy

What abra-ca-dabra.app and the Readiness Check collect, why, and how to reach us.

Effective date: [to be confirmed]

This website

abra-ca-dabra.app is a static website hosted on Cloudflare. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and the site itself does not set cookies. Like any host, Cloudflare processes standard request data, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and protect the site.

The site has no forms. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you include, and we use it only to reply.

The Readiness Check

The Readiness Check at check.abra-ca-dabra.app stores the answers you give so it can score them and show your results. If you choose to, it also stores your name, your email address and your website address. We use your website address to run a public scan of that site. We use your email address to send your report and, if you book a working session, notes from that session. Nothing else is sent.

To prevent abuse, the check keeps a one-way hash of your IP address rather than the address itself. Links from this site into the check can carry a label showing which page you came from.

The check educates and points to a next step. It is not medical, legal or financial advice, and it does not diagnose.

Analytics

We may add analytics (PostHog) in the future, and only after you consent. No analytics run today.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to houdini@abra-ca-dabra.app.
