The name

I will create as I speak.

Abracadabra, traced back far enough, is Aramaic, roughly avra kadavra. Not a spell. You describe the work, and it becomes real software.

HQ product video

A 56-second silent video about HQ. Every request lands as a card on the work board and becomes a written plan before work starts. A team of digital workers, which are AI agents, picks up the work, searches the skill library for each task, uses the skill packs, cites the skills used and reports whether they helped. Client Services reviews before anything reaches you, and finished work lands in your folders and Library. Only client-approved finished work can be scheduled or posted: once a piece is approved and your accounts are connected, it is scheduled into your next open slot, for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, YouTube, Threads, Google Business and Bluesky. HQ runs on Cloudflare. The video ends with the Readiness Check, a five-to-seven-minute questionnaire.

HQ

One place to request, review and approve the work.

HQ is the workspace where your Abracadabra team and a crew of digital workers deliver. Nothing reaches your folders or your social networks until it has been reviewed and you have approved it. It is for CEOs and business owners leveraging AI across their businesses.

  1. Request it

    Each request becomes a card on your board, with a plan for the work.

  2. Review it

    Finished work is held, checked by Client Services, then released for you to approve, change or remove.

  3. Schedule it

    Once a piece is approved and your accounts are connected, it is scheduled into your next open slot. Networks: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, YouTube, Threads, Google Business and Bluesky.

Questions

What does Abracadabra AI build?

We design, build and run AI-powered tools that fit your business. AI handles the busywork, and you keep the customer relationship. Recent work includes Milrcorr, the StrongFoam website, Schema, LLM Leverage and Super Funnel.

Do you only build software?

No. We can also handle the marketing, the website, and getting you found, all built around how you work.

What does the name Abracadabra mean?

Traced back far enough, Abracadabra is Aramaic, roughly avra kadavra: I will create as I speak. Describe how your business works, and we turn it into real, running software, not a mockup.

Do you have results to show?

pirx.ca went from near-zero to 398K search impressions and 46.5K AI-feature impressions in 90 days (Google Search Console, Jul 18 to Oct 17, 2026).

How does a project work?

We learn how the work gets done today, build the software around it, and launch it with you. You keep the system, plus a short film and script to show it off.

Who is Abracadabra AI for?

Businesses whose work doesn’t fit off-the-shelf software, or who are tired of juggling tools that each hold a piece of the customer.

What if a product I can buy already does the job?

Buy it. When it does not fit, we build the one that does.

Are the wellness assessments you build diagnostic?

No. The clinical-style assessments we have shipped are non-diagnostic. They educate and route a person to a next step. They do not diagnose.

How do I know if my business is ready to put AI to work?

Take the Readiness Check. It shows where the work slows down: whether the data, the workflow, the owner, and the decision can support real work, and how visible the public site is to answer engines. Five to seven minutes, then a thirty-minute working session if you want one.
