Questions

What does Abracadabra AI build?

We design, build and run AI-powered tools that fit your business. AI takes a task. You keep the relationship. You leave with the live system, a film of the screens, and the words you use to show it. Shipped examples include Milrcorr, the StrongFoam website, Schema, LLM Leverage, and an associate app shown here as the Getting started film (Super Funnel).

Do you only build software?

No. Marketing, the application, the model, the customer record, and the work that gets you found ship with the product, fitted to how you already work. Public examples on this page include Milrcorr, StrongFoam, Schema, LLM Leverage, Super Funnel, and PIRX. Other work is named when you allow it.

What does the name Abracadabra mean?

Traced back far enough, Abracadabra is Aramaic, roughly avra kadavra: I will create as I speak. The steps, the exceptions, and the words you already use become a running system. The creation is production software, not a mockup.

What is the proof behind from zero to scale?

From near-zero to 375K search impressions and 43.5K AI-feature impressions in 90 days. Google Search Console, Jul 14 to Oct 13, 2026, recorded 375K search impressions, 1.85K clicks, and an average position of 7.6 for pirx.ca. The Generative AI features report is a separate count of 43.5K impressions and is not added to the search total. Both curves start near zero in mid-July.

How does a project work?

You tell us the steps, the exceptions, and the sentence a good rep already says. We build the software around those steps, with AI where it removes a task and a person where the relationship matters. You leave with the live system, the film, and the script, and you can demo it without us in the room.

Who is Abracadabra AI for?

Your business, when the way you work does not fit a product you can buy. You already have customers and a way of working no off-the-shelf product was drawn for. Or you are replacing a pile of tools that each hold a piece of the customer. Or you need the product and the way you sell, in the same delivery. Or the job is too specific for a catalog, and we take it.

What if a product I can buy already does the job?

Buy it. When it does not fit, we build the one that does.

Are the wellness assessments you build diagnostic?

No. The clinical-style assessments we have shipped are non-diagnostic. They educate and route a person to a next step. They do not diagnose.

How do I know if my business is ready to put AI to work?

Take the Readiness Check at https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check. It shows where the work slows down: whether the data, the workflow, the owner, and the decision can support real work, and how visible the public site is to answer engines. Five to seven minutes, then a thirty-minute working session if you want one.
