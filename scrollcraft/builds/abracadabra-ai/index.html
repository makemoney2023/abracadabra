Questions

What does Abracadabra AI build?

We design, build and run AI-powered tools that fit your business. AI handles the busywork, and you keep the customer relationship. Recent work includes Milrcorr, the StrongFoam website, Schema, LLM Leverage and Super Funnel.

Do you only build software?

No. We can also handle the marketing, the website, and getting you found, all built around how you work.

What does the name Abracadabra mean?

Traced back far enough, Abracadabra is Aramaic, roughly avra kadavra: I will create as I speak. Describe how your business works, and we turn it into real, running software, not a mockup.

Do you have results to show?

pirx.ca went from near-zero to 375K search impressions and 43.5K AI-feature impressions in 90 days (Google Search Console, Jul 14 to Oct 13, 2026).

How does a project work?

We learn how the work gets done today, build the software around it, and launch it with you. You keep the system, plus a short film and script to show it off.

Who is Abracadabra AI for?

Businesses whose work doesn't fit off-the-shelf software, or who are tired of juggling tools that each hold a piece of the customer.

What if a product I can buy already does the job?

Buy it. When it does not fit, we build the one that does.

Are the wellness assessments you build diagnostic?

No. The clinical-style assessments we have shipped are non-diagnostic. They educate and route a person to a next step. They do not diagnose.

How do I know if my business is ready to put AI to work?

Take the Readiness Check. It shows where the work slows down: whether the data, the workflow, the owner, and the decision can support real work, and how visible the public site is to answer engines. Five to seven minutes, then a thirty-minute working session if you want one.
