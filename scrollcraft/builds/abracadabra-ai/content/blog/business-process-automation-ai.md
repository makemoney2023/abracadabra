Business process automation with AI starts as a written job. Anthropic's December 2024 essay says to start with the simplest solution that works, and to prefer a fixed workflow until the next step actually depends on the last one.[^anthropic]

The [process guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/fix/ready-process-mid) is the matching fix. The [automation note](/blog/ai-automation-for-small-business) is which job to pick. The [agents note](/blog/ai-agents-for-business) is when a workflow is not enough.

## The process is the product

Microsoft's 2024 index says 75% of knowledge workers use AI, and 46% of those users started less than six months earlier.[^microsoft] 60% of leaders worry leadership lacks a plan and vision. 78% of AI users bring their own tools, and 80% do so at small and midsize businesses. 60% of time in the work apps was spent in email, chat, and meetings, and 40% in creation apps. Only 25% of companies were planning generative AI training. 55% of leaders were concerned about talent.

<!--image:2-->

Gartner's January 2026 article says at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept by the end of the prior year, citing poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs, and unclear value.[^gartner-fail]

<!--cta:inline-->

Write the steps a person already follows. Mark the step that waits for a judgment. Automate the steps that do not. If every step needs a judgment, you do not have a process yet.
