llms.txt is a proposed text file at the root of a site. Jeremy Howard published the proposal in September 2024, and the page was updated in August 2026.[^llmstxt] The idea is a short list of the pages you want a model to read. This site already publishes [its own file](/llms.txt). That is a fact about this site, not a traffic statistic.

Google's guide says Google does not use llms.txt for generative features in search.[^google]

The [missing-file guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/fix/missing-llms-txt) is the check. The [answer-engine note](/blog/answer-engine-optimization) is what Google does require. The [GEO comparison](/blog/generative-engine-optimization-vs-seo) is the research.

## What a crawl of the files found

Ahrefs, in a study published in June 2026, looked at 137,210 domains in its web analytics that had traffic in May 2026.[^ahrefs] 28% published an llms.txt file, about 38,000 valid files. 97% of those files received zero requests in May. 96% of the requests that did arrive were bots. 19.5% of requests came from named AI tools, and 12% from audit or scanner tools.

Publish the file if you want a clean list of your pages. Do not expect Google to read it. Keep the pages indexed and worth citing either way.
