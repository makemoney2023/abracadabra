An AI pilot stalls when the demo worked and the process did not. Gartner's January 2026 article says at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept, because of poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs, or unclear value.[^gartner-fail]

The [decision guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/fix/ready-decision-low) is the matching fix. The [implementation framework](/blog/ai-implementation-framework) is the sequence. The [data note](/blog/ai-ready-data) is the first failure.

## Use is not scaling

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 88% of respondents report regular AI use in at least one function, up from 78% a year earlier.[^mckinsey] About one third of organizations are scaling. 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are experimenting. No more than 10% are scaling agents in any one function. 29% of firms under $100 million in revenue are at the scaling stage, against nearly half of firms over $5 billion.

<!--image:2-->

Gartner said in June 2025 that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027.[^gartner-agentic]

<!--cta:inline-->

A pilot that cannot name the data, the checker, and the value is already in the abandoned pile. Move it into a written process or stop it.
