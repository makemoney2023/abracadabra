An AI consultant names the job, the data, and the stop. An automation agency builds the system after that naming is done. Hiring the agency first produces a build with no owner. The [consultant note](/blog/ai-consultant-for-small-business) is the question list. The [buy-or-build note](/blog/off-the-shelf-ai-or-custom-software) is whether to build at all. The [decision guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/fix/ready-decision-mid) is the matching score.

## Two training figures, two populations

Microsoft's 2024 index says only 25% of companies were planning to offer generative AI training.[^microsoft] Statistics Canada's 2025 brief says 38.9% of Canadian businesses that already use AI had trained staff.[^statcan] Those are different populations. One is a plan among companies. The other is training among businesses that already use AI. Do not average them.

<!--image:2-->

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are experimenting.[^mckinsey] An agency pitch that quotes only a use figure is selling the experiment.

<!--cta:inline-->

Pay a consultant to refuse the work that a product already does. Pay an agency when the process is written and the checker is named.
