Two surveys from 2025 count AI in small firms, and they count different work. The U.S. Chamber asked who uses generative AI. Statistics Canada asked who used AI to produce goods or deliver services. This note keeps those claims apart, then names the jobs owners hand over. The [pressure guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/pressure) is the matching question for one business. [Christopher Buzny](/about/christopher-buzny) writes these notes from the [studio site](/).

## Generative use is not production use

The Chamber's Empowering Small Business report says 58% of small businesses self-identified that they use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.[^chamber] That question asks about generative AI. It does not ask whether AI produces the goods or the service.

Statistics Canada, in the Canadian Survey on Business Conditions for the second quarter of 2025, found that 12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% in the second quarter of 2024.[^statcan] The release is dated 16 June 2025. Collection ran from 1 April to 5 May 2025. The agency received 21,357 responses, of which 9,103 came from businesses that used AI.

<!--image:2-->

Among businesses that used AI, text analytics accounted for 35.7%, and marketing automation for 23.1%. 40.1% had developed new workflows, and 38.9% had trained staff.

<!--cta:inline-->

## Jobs an owner can hand over

The Chamber figure describes a tool someone opens. The StatCan figure describes AI inside the delivery of the product or the service. A small business can do the first for a long time without the second.

The jobs that show up in the StatCan breakdown are the practical list: reading and sorting text, sending and measuring marketing, writing a workflow that did not exist, and training the people who will run it. None of those is a promise that the tool will run the company.

The [readiness note](/blog/ai-readiness-assessment) asks a different question: whether this firm, not the average firm, has the data, the process, the people, and the decision in place.
