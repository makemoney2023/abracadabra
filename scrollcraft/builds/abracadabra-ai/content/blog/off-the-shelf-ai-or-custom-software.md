Off-the-shelf AI is the right buy when a product already does the job. Custom software is the right build when the job is specific to how this business works, and no product covers it. The [studio](/) takes the work that products do not cover. The [consultant note](/blog/ai-consultant-for-small-business) is what to ask before either path. The [agency comparison](/blog/ai-automation-agency-vs-consultant) is who you hire.

## What the surveys count

The Chamber's 2025 report says 58% of small businesses use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.[^chamber] That is a use claim. It is not a claim that those firms built custom software.

<!--image:2-->

Salesforce surveyed 3,350 leaders at firms of 200 employees and under.[^salesforce] 75% were at least experimenting. 83% of growing small and midsize businesses said that. 91% of those already using AI said it boosts revenue. The survey does not say whether the tool was bought or built.

<!--cta:inline-->

If a bought product covers the job, buy it. If the job depends on your records, your exceptions, and your checker, the product will not invent those. That is the custom case.
