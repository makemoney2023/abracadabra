Generative engine optimization and SEO are not rival crafts in Google's guide. Google says answer-engine optimization and generative-engine optimization are still SEO.[^google] The page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet. Google does not use llms.txt. There is no special chunking requirement.

The [visibility guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/visibility) is the check. The [answer-engine note](/blog/answer-engine-optimization) is the tactic list. The [llms.txt note](/blog/what-is-llms-txt) is the file Google says it ignores.

## What changes, and what does not

The 2024 paper reports visibility gains of up to 40% when the page is written for a generative answer.[^geo] Citations, quotations, and statistics were associated with gains over 40%. Perplexity showed gains up to 37%. Position-adjusted word count rose 41%, and subjective impression rose 28%, on a benchmark of 10,000 queries.

Pew found, in July 2025, that a visit with an AI summary was followed by a click 8% of the time, against 15% with no summary.[^pew] SEO still gets the page indexed. The generative layer still has to be worth the click.
