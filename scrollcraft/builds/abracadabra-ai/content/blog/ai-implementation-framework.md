An implementation framework is the order of work after you know the job. NIST names Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.[^nist] This note uses five stages in words: name the job, score the data, write the process, assign a checker, then keep or stop.

The [Forming band](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/band/forming) is where many businesses sit while this is unfinished. The [stall note](/blog/why-ai-pilots-stall) is what happens if you skip a stage. The [readiness framework](/blog/ai-readiness-framework) is the score that comes first.

## What the numbers say about the middle

McKinsey's 2025 survey says 88% report regular AI use in at least one function.[^mckinsey] 23% are scaling an agentic system somewhere, and 39% are experimenting. 29% of firms under $100 million are scaling, against nearly half of firms over $5 billion. No more than 10% scale agents in any one function.

<!--image:2-->

Cisco's 2025 survey of 8,000 leaders found pacesetters, near 13% of that sample, were 4 times more likely to move a pilot into production.[^cisco] Those leaders work in organizations with more than 500 employees. A small business can still copy the sequence: a written process, then production.

<!--cta:inline-->

Stop at the stage you cannot finish. A checker you cannot name means you are not ready to keep the work.
