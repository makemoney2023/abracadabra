An AI agent, in Anthropic's December 2024 essay, is a system that directs its own tools toward a goal.[^anthropic] A workflow is the opposite: the steps are written in advance. Agentic AI is the category vendors use when they mean many such systems, often across a company. The words are not interchangeable.

The [definition](/blog/what-is-an-ai-agent) is the short version. The [jobs list](/blog/ai-agents-for-business) is where an agent might sit. The [people guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/fix/ready-people-mid) is who has to check the output.

## What the forecast says

Gartner said in June 2025 that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027.[^gartner-agentic] The reasons are cost, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls. The same note said 15% of day-to-day work decisions will be made autonomously by 2028, up from 0% in 2024, and that 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI by 2028, up from less than 1% in 2024.

If you can write the steps, you want a workflow. If the next step depends on what the last step found, you may want an agent, and you still want a person who can stop it. Calling that agentic does not change the checkpoint.
