Getting a business to show up in ChatGPT search starts with the crawler that search actually uses. OpenAI documents separate crawlers.[^openai] OAI-SearchBot fetches pages for search results. Opting that crawler out keeps the pages out of ChatGPT search answers. GPTBot is the training crawler. ChatGPT-User fetches when a person triggers a look-up, and robots.txt may not apply to it. They are not one switch.

The [visibility guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/visibility) reads the site. The [answer-engine note](/blog/answer-engine-optimization) and the [generative-engine note](/blog/generative-engine-optimization-vs-seo) stay on the vocabulary.

## What Google says to do

Google's guide for generative features says the work is still SEO.[^google] It says answer-engine optimization and generative-engine optimization are still SEO from Google's point of view. It says Google does not use llms.txt. It says there is no special chunking requirement. The page has to be indexed, and it has to be eligible for a snippet. No extra schema is required for those features.

<!--image:2-->

A page that is blocked, thin, or ineligible for a snippet will not become an overview because a new file was added. Allow the search crawler, keep the page indexable, and write the answer where a person can also read it.

<!--cta:inline-->

Pew, in July 2025, found that visits with an AI summary produced a click 8% of the time, against 15% when no summary appeared.[^pew] Showing up is not the same as being clicked. Write the page so the citation is worth the click.
