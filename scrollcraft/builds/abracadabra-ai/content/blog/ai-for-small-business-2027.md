The useful question for a small business is still which job changes. Two 2025 surveys remain the clean public counts, and they do not measure the same thing.

The [help note](/blog/how-can-ai-help-small-businesses) is the full split. The [strategy note](/blog/ai-strategy-for-small-business) is what to do with the gap. The [assessment](/blog/ai-readiness-assessment) is how to score one firm.

## Two questions, two answers

The Chamber's 2025 report says 58% of small businesses use generative AI, up from 40% in 2024 and 23% in 2023.[^chamber]

<!--image:2-->

Statistics Canada, for 2025, found 12.2% of businesses used AI to produce goods or deliver services, up from 6.1% a year earlier.[^statcan]

<!--cta:inline-->

A firm can say yes to the Chamber question and no to the Statistics Canada question. Plan from the job you can name. Do not average the two percentages into a single readiness rate. This site does not publish an aggregate score from the Readiness Check.
