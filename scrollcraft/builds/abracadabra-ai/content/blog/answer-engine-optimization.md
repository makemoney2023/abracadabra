Answer engine optimization is the work of being cited when a generative answer is written. Google's guide says that work is still SEO.[^google] The page has to be indexed, and it has to be eligible for a snippet. Google says it does not use llms.txt, and it says there is no special chunking requirement. No extra schema is required for those features.

The [visibility guide](https://check.abra-ca-dabra.app/check/guide/visibility) is the matching check. The [ChatGPT note](/blog/how-to-get-your-business-to-show-up-in-chatgpt) covers crawler files. The [GEO comparison](/blog/generative-engine-optimization-vs-seo) is the research split.

## What the research measured

A 2024 paper on generative engine optimization reports visibility gains of up to 40%.[^geo] Methods that add citations, quotations, and statistics were associated with gains over 40%. On Perplexity the reported gain reached 37%. Position-adjusted word count rose 41%, and a subjective impression score rose 28%. The benchmark used 10,000 queries.

Pew, in July 2025, found that visits with an AI summary produced a click 8% of the time, against 15% when no summary appeared.[^pew] A citation that nobody clicks is still a citation. Write the page so the click is worth taking.
